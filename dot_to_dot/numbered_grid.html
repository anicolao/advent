What this trial changes

38 Sudoku cells unlock the tile’s 38 strokes. Every dot has one unique number. Revisited junctions move to nearby unused positions, introducing small gaps; compare with “Original grid drawing”. All 3,721 positions are labelled, so zoom is needed on screen. Try the SVG at A2 size for print legibility.

Wrong digits unlock 304 decoy paths on otherwise unused positions. Decoy shapes are random walks, not equally convincing alternative pictures. This tests the interaction; it does not prove concealment or establish Sudoku difficulty. The demo includes the answer data.
