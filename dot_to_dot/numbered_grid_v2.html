Drawing rules and changes

The drawing sheet has sectors A–F across and 1–6 down. An entry C2 / 812 / +6 means find 812 in sector C2, then connect through 818. This is a notation example, not an answer.

Each dot has two sequence numbers. Both refer to the same point; follow only the number you need. Shared junctions stay exact. The next dot is always within half a sector’s width (in fact at most 0.3 sector).

966 dots, two labels each. Intermediate points stay exactly on the original lines. The background is now slightly irregular; concealment needs another review. The SVG drawing square is about 348 mm wide: the longest step is about 17.4 mm.
